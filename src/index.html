Designed around your downloads.

Downloads are part of everyday life. Ratatoskr is being built to give them a place of their own, with a focused desktop interface and a visual identity worth keeping open.

Its name comes from the squirrel of Norse mythology. Here, the squirrel becomes a companion for the files moving through your day.

What is Ratatoskr?+

Ratatoskr is a desktop download manager in development. This page introduces its identity and interface design. Confirmed functionality will be documented alongside the app’s releases.

Can I download it now?+

Check the release section above for current availability. When the first public builds are ready, that section will include the download files and release notes.

Why the squirrel?+

Ratatoskr is named after a squirrel from Norse mythology. The squirrel, tree motifs and runic details give the project a distinct visual character.

Are these real screenshots?+

The images currently shown are interface design previews. They will be replaced with screenshots from the finished application as development progresses.
